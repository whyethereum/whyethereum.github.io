About Why Ethereum

An independent, plain-English guide to Ethereum for people who are new to it, or just short on time.

What we do

Ethereum's developers make big decisions on public calls and in long technical threads. We turn those decisions into short, clear updates you can read in a minute, and we keep a running tracker of every upgrade.

How we cover news

We check the site every day. Each story links to the sources behind it, starting with primary ones: All Core Developers call notes, Ethereum Foundation posts and official roadmap pages. When we rely on a news outlet, we name it.

We report setbacks as plainly as progress. If an upgrade slips, you'll read about it here.

Partners and money

The site is free to read. If we ever earn money from a link or partner, we label it clearly. Partners never see or influence our news coverage, and we never promise price moves.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the correction on the page where it appeared. Spotted a mistake? Open an issue on our GitHub page.

Not financial advice

Everything here is for education. Crypto is volatile, and you should only invest what you can afford to lose.

Why Ethereum is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with the Ethereum Foundation or with any exchange.
