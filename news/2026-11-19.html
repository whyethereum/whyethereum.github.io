Ethereum news: November 19, 2026

Glamsterdam gets extra safety checks, Hegotá locks in two features, Devcon 8 wraps up, and the quantum-safe roadmap.

Upgrade

Glamsterdam upgrade gets extra safety checks

The next upgrade is live on the Sepolia test network. Developers paused its next test rollout to review how new gas prices affect existing smart contracts, with outside auditors involved. Mainnet is now expected in early 2027.

Sources: Bankless, Cointelegraph

Roadmap

Hegotá upgrade locks in two key features

The upgrade after Glamsterdam will add stronger censorship resistance (FOCIL) and smarter, easier wallets (frame transactions). Developers say they'll delay rather than ship without either one.

Sources: ethereum.org, ForkLog

Community

Devcon 8 wraps up in Mumbai

Ethereum's flagship developer conference ran November 3–6, its first in India. Talks focused on post-quantum security, privacy and verifiable hardware. Next up is EthCC in Cannes, April 12–15, 2027.

Source: Crypto Briefing

Security

Quantum-safe Ethereum targeted for 2029

The long-term "Lean Ethereum" plan aims for full protection against quantum computers by December 2029, plus much faster finality and higher throughput. A progress review is due in January 2027.

Source: Ethereum Foundation post-quantum roadmap
